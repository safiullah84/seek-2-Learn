Help Center

Find answers to frequently asked questions or reach out to our support team.

Getting Started

How do I create an account? ▼
Click the "Sign Up Free" button on any page. Enter your name, email, and password. You'll be ready to start learning in under 60 seconds — no credit card required.
Is Seek2Learn free to use? ▼
We offer a Free Explorer plan with access to 3 courses, community forums, and basic progress tracking. For unlimited access, certificates, and live sessions, check our Pro plan starting at ₹999/month.
Can I access courses on mobile? ▼
Yes! Seek2Learn works on any device and is fully responsive. Our mobile app (coming soon) will also support offline downloads so you can learn on the go.

Courses & Learning

How long do I have access to a course? ▼
All courses come with lifetime access. Once enrolled, you can revisit the material anytime, including all future updates to the course content.
Can I download course videos? ▼
Pro and Team plan subscribers can download course videos for offline viewing through our mobile app. Free plan users can stream all content online.
What if I get stuck on a lesson? ▼
You can ask questions in the course discussion forum, join our weekly live Q&A sessions, or reach out to our support team. Our community of 50,000+ learners is always ready to help.

Billing & Refunds

What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and PayPal. For Team plans, we also support invoicing and wire transfers.
Can I get a refund? ▼
Yes! We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans and individual course purchases. Contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.
How do I cancel my subscription? ▼
You can cancel anytime from your account settings. Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees.

Still Need Help?

Our support team is available Mon–Fri, 9 AM – 6 PM IST. Average response time: under 4 hours.
