Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch plans later? ▼
Yes! You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time from your dashboard. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a money-back guarantee? ▼
Absolutely. We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all paid plans. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund — no questions asked.
Do you offer student discounts? ▼
Yes! Students with a valid .edu email get 40% off all plans. Contact us with your student ID to claim your discount.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI, net banking, and PayPal. For Team plans, we also support invoicing and wire transfers.
