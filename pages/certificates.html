Seek2Learn Certificate of Completion

Each certificate is uniquely generated with a verification code, your name, course title, completion date, and instructor signature.

How Certificates Work

  1. Complete all lessons — watch every video and complete all assignments in your course.
  2. Pass the final assessment — score 70% or higher on the course's final exam or capstone project.
  3. Download your certificate — instantly download a beautiful, branded PDF certificate.
  4. Share with the world — add it to LinkedIn, your resume, or your portfolio with one click.

Certificate Features

  • Unique verification code — employers can verify your certificate's authenticity online.
  • Blockchain-secured — certificates are recorded on a public ledger for tamper-proof verification.
  • LinkedIn integration — share directly to your LinkedIn profile with a single click.
  • Professional design — beautifully designed certificates that look great printed or digital.
  • Lifetime access — download your certificate anytime from your dashboard.
