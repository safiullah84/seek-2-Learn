Company Overview

Seek2Learn is an online learning platform founded in 2023 that offers expert-led, career-focused courses in technology, design, marketing, and data science. With over 50,000 active learners across 40+ countries, Seek2Learn is on a mission to make quality education accessible and career-transforming.

Key Facts

  • Founders: Aisha Khan (CEO), Rohan Verma (CTO)
  • Active Learners: 50,000+

  • Instructors: 200+ industry experts
  • Completion Rate: 98%
  • Countries: 40+
